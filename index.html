How to play
The grid looks like a valid sudoku, but several cells were secretly altered. Easier difficulties play smaller grids: 4×4 for Warm-up, 6×6 for Classic, the full 9×9 beyond that. Flag every cell you believe is lying: +100 per lie caught, −25 per false accusation (max −50 a board). On Hard the lies never share a row, column, or box. On Ruthless the lies are swaps: every row and box stays valid, so only the columns give them away.
